# My Favorite Series: Naruto

## About
**Naruto** is a Japanese *anime* and *manga* series created by **Masashi Kishimoto**. It follows Naruto Uzumaki, a loud and mischievous boy who is shunned by his village because a powerful fox spirit is sealed inside him. Despite being lonely, he dreams of becoming the **Hokage** so everyone will respect him.

Along with his teammates **Sasuke** and **Sakura**, and guided by their teacher **Kakashi**, Naruto trains to become a strong ninja. He faces dangerous enemies, makes lifelong friends, and works to bring back Sasuke, who leaves the village to chase power.

The story shows that hard work, loyalty, and refusing to give up can help anyone overcome their past.

## Top 3 Characters
1. **Obito Uchiha** - a tragic character with a deep backstory
2. **Naruto Uzumaki** - the energetic, never-give-up hero
3. **Sasuke Uchiha** - Naruto's rival and best friend

## Why I Like It
- Great story about hard work and never giving up
- Exciting fights and ninja techniques
- Memorable characters and friendships
- Emotional moments and strong themes
